# Portadas Neón Costero

- Origen de la dirección de arte. Paletas XV y 18 del ZIP `Pixvo_GTA_VI_15_Nenas_18_Nenes_Kit.zip`, aportado por el titular. SHA-256 del ZIP `0b53501bf86f5a873c965c5e64e56dc70b3b75ce909f003f3a68a68527515336`.
- Arte. Ilustración generada para Pixvo con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Del original se recortaron dos portadas independientes de 768 × 1024 y se exportaron como WebP.
- Uso. Portadas del catálogo, la portada comercial, vistas previas, editor y eventos publicados de Pixvo. Los personajes son diseños originales. La documentación del kit indica que el arte de referencia de la franquicia requiere autorización para explotación comercial.
- Integridad. SHA-256 de ambos archivos en `public/assets/provenance.json`, generado por `scripts/sync-presets.mjs`.

## Consigna de generación

> Create a landscape 3:2 illustration diptych for a digital birthday invitation website. Two equally sized independent VERTICAL poster backgrounds, side by side split at exactly 50%, no gap or borders. Each half will be cropped into its own separate poster. Leave the top 34% of EACH half quiet and dark for placed white text, and the center-left of each half relatively subdued as well. Left panel: an original adult young Afro-Latina woman with two long braids threaded in gold, lilac moto jacket and gold star earrings, shown waist up, face at approx 62% from top, position to the right of that panel; a retro coastal city at sundown, silhouetted palms, coral sun, saturated hot pink #FF4B9A, lilac #D2B1FA, coral #FF988F, deep plum #130C23. Right panel: an original adult young man with medium brown skin, short curly hair with an electric-blue streak, square aqua glasses and a loose cream and teal bowling shirt, shown waist up, face similarly at approx 62% from top, positioned to the right of that panel, blue-hour seaside street, palms, Art Deco architecture, cyan #3DE1E1, twilight blue #578CFB, tangerine #FF9D6B and navy #09111F. Two new independent characters with no resemblance to existing media characters. Sophisticated vintage Miami travel poster meets modern graphic novel, confident ink silhouettes, atmospheric halftones, saturated screenprinted color fields and cinematic lighting. No text, no letters, no logos, no insignia, no firearms, no watermark.
